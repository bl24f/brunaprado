Project Overview

Tracked and analyzed Instagram and LinkedIn engagement metrics across 12 Florida universities over 7 weeks. The project involved building a robust Excel tracking system from scratch, collecting data at Wednesday and Sunday checkpoints, and identifying patterns in what content drives the highest engagement for university accounts.

Key Contributions
  • Built an Excel workbook with Summary, Raw Tracking Data, and Dashboard tabs with automated SUMIF formulas
  • Collected 80+ data entries per platform across Wednesday and Sunday checkpoints over 7 weeks
  • Resolved complex data issues including trailing-space mismatches causing SUMIF lookup failures
  • Identified top-performing content types and optimal posting schedules for university social accounts
  • Delivered data-driven recommendations in a structured group presentation covering slides 11-17
