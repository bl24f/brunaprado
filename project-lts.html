Project Overview

Developed a complete social media campaign for LTS Studio, a modern streetwear brand built around comfort, confidence, and individuality. The campaign required building the brand voice from scratch, defining target audience personas, and creating a content strategy to grow engagement organically across Instagram and Twitter.

Key Contributions
  • Defined brand tone, personality, and content pillars aligned with LTS Studio's streetwear identity
  • Created detailed target audience personas based on demographics, psychographics, and shopping behavior
  • Developed platform-specific content calendars for Instagram and Twitter with copy and visual direction
  • Analyzed 5+ competitor accounts and proposed differentiation strategies based on engagement data
  • Designed a hashtag strategy and engagement loop to drive community interaction
